Privacy Policy

Ariel Merino Chong · Miami, Florida · Last updated 14 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ariel Merino Chong (the registered business, referred to here as "we") collects, uses and protects personal information in connection with the missed-call text-back service provided to small local businesses in Florida.

What we collect

When you call a business that uses our service and your call is not answered, or when you write to that business through its website chat, we collect:

  • The mobile phone number you called from
  • The content of the messages you exchange with the business
  • The name and details you choose to provide, such as what you need, a preferred appointment time, and, depending on the business, your service address, your insurance carrier or your vehicle
  • The date and time of each message

We do not ask for, and you should not send, Social Security numbers, dates of birth, insurance member identifiers, or card or bank details.

How we use it

  • To reply to the enquiry you started by calling or writing to the business
  • To pass your request to that business so its staff can contact you and arrange an appointment
  • To keep a record of the conversation so the business can serve you properly

We do not use your information to send you promotional or marketing messages.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Who can see your information

Your messages and details are visible to the local business you contacted and to Ariel Merino Chong as the operator of the service. We use Twilio to deliver text messages and Anthropic to generate replies; both act as service providers and process your data only to provide this service. We disclose information otherwise only where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Conversations and contact details are kept for as long as the business needs them to serve you, and are deleted on request.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts from that business. Reply START to resume.
  • Reply HELP for contact details and help.
  • Write to us at the address below to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies and depends on your own conversation. Message and data rates may apply.

Children

This service is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
